Activity 03

Team Leader Prompt Architect: The RTC Framework

Transform ineffective leadership prompts into high-precision, balanced prompts using the Role, Task, and Context model.

The Leadership Challenge

A manager entered this unrefined prompt: "Write a message telling my team to stop leaving dirty coffee mugs in the sink." The AI generated a hostile, four-paragraph disciplinary threat that upset the entire team. As an operational Team Leader, re-architect this prompt using the RTC Formula to achieve compliance without damaging psychological safety.

Step 1

Define the Role (R)

Who is the AI pretending to be? (Sets emotional intelligence and team relationship baseline).

⚠️ Wrong Persona: This persona causes the LLM to default to punitive, adversarial legalisms that undermine team morale.
✅ Ideal Persona: Establishes a balanced, empathetic peer-level leadership baseline that encourages shared ownership.
Step 2

Define the Task (T)

What exact asset and channel are you delivering?

✅ Proportionate Deliverable: Fast, modern channel-appropriate communication that respects employee time.
⚠️ Disproportionate Escalation: Escalating a dirty mug into a formal policy memo creates bureaucratic fatigue.
Step 3

Define Context & Constraints (C)

What are the guardrails, length limits, and cultural standards?

✅ Strict Guardrails: Explicit word limits and tone instructions prevent the LLM from hallucinating defensive or accusatory phrases.
⚠️ Destructive Constraints: Threat-based constraints dismantle team trust and produce hostile messages.

Master Prompt Formula: Role + Task + Context = Focused, High-Impact Leadership Output.
