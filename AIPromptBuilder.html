Activity 03

Team Leader Prompt Architect: RTC Challenge

Transform vague operational requests into high-precision, balanced prompts using the Role–Task–Context (RTC) model.

Task Overview & Instructions

Unrefined prompts force AI word predictors to guess operational tone and scope, frequently generating adversarial or corporate-bloated outputs. Team leaders must craft structured prompts that provide clear operational direction while protecting psychological safety and team culture.

What you must do:
  1. Read the workplace problem statement below.
  2. Review the Target RTC Requirements (Role, Task, and Context parameters).
  3. Type your complete prompt into the input workspace.
  4. Click Evaluate My Prompt to check your coverage against the RTC criteria.
  5. Once you make an attempt, the Reveal Model Prompt button will activate to compare your wording against the exemplar.

The Operational Bottleneck

A manager entered this unrefined prompt: "Write a message telling my team to stop leaving dirty coffee mugs in the sink." The AI produced an aggressive, four-paragraph disciplinary warning that damaged team morale. Your mission is to write a prompt that achieves operational cleanliness without creating friction.

R

Role

Must direct the AI to act as an approachable team leader or supervisor.

T

Task

Must direct the AI to draft a short team channel update/reminder (Slack/Teams) about kitchen cleanliness.

C

Context & Constraints

Must enforce a friendly/warm tone and a word or sentence limit (e.g., max 60 words or 3 sentences).

Evaluation Result

    Model Benchmark Prompt

    "Act as an approachable, supportive operational team leader. Draft a concise 3-sentence team channel reminder (Slack/Teams) asking the team to rinse and load their mugs into the dishwasher. Keep the tone warm, lighthearted, and focused on collective pride in our shared space, with a maximum length of 60 words."

    Notice how explicit boundaries prevent the AI from generating defensive rhetoric or formal reprimands.

    Master Prompt Formula: Role + Task + Context = Focused, High-Impact Leadership Output.
