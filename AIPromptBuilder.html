Activity 03

The Role–Task–Context (RTC) Prompt Builder

Transform ineffective prompts by selecting the ideal components under the RTC prompting framework.

The Problem: A manager entered: "Write a message telling my team to stop leaving dirty coffee mugs in the sink." The result was a punitive, 4-paragraph diatribe that damaged team morale.

1. Select the Role (R)

Who is the AI pretending to be?

⚠️ This persona will cause the model to write adversarial, punitive copy.
✅ Excellent. Sets an empathetic, peer-level leadership baseline.

2. Select the Task (T)

What exact deliverable do you need built?

✅ Concise, clear deliverable matched to daily communication channels.
⚠️ Disproportionate response that turns a kitchen reminder into red tape.

3. Select Context & Constraints (C)

What are the guardrails, length, and tone boundaries?

✅ Enforces strict word limits and preserves psychological safety.
⚠️ Threat-focused constraints produce confrontational communications.

Master Prompt Formula: Role + Task + Context = Focused, Safe Workplace Outputs.
