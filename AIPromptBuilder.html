Activity 03

Team Leader Prompt Architect: RTC Challenge

Transform vague operational requests into high-precision, balanced prompts using the Role–Task–Context (RTC) model[cite: 13, 14].

Task Overview & Instructions

Unrefined prompts force AI word predictors to guess operational tone and scope, frequently generating adversarial or corporate-bloated outputs[cite: 8, 14]. Team leaders must craft structured prompts that provide clear operational direction while protecting psychological safety and team culture[cite: 2, 9, 13].

What you must do:
  1. Read the workplace problem statement below[cite: 14].
  2. Review the Target RTC Requirements (Role, Task, and Context parameters)[cite: 13, 14].
  3. Type your complete prompt into the input workspace[cite: 14].
  4. Click Evaluate My Prompt to check your coverage against the RTC criteria[cite: 13, 14].
  5. Once you make an attempt, the Reveal Model Prompt button will activate to compare your wording against the exemplar[cite: 14].

The Operational Bottleneck

A manager entered this unrefined prompt: "Write a message telling my team to stop leaving dirty coffee mugs in the sink."[cite: 14] The AI produced an aggressive, four-paragraph disciplinary warning that damaged team morale[cite: 14]. Your mission is to write a prompt that achieves operational cleanliness without creating friction[cite: 13, 14].

R

Role[cite: 13, 14]

Must direct the AI to act as an approachable team leader or supervisor[cite: 13, 14].

T

Task[cite: 13, 14]

Must direct the AI to draft a short team channel update/reminder (Slack/Teams) about kitchen cleanliness[cite: 13, 14].

C

Context & Constraints[cite: 13, 14]

Must enforce a friendly/warm tone and a word or sentence limit (e.g., max 60 words or 3 sentences)[cite: 13, 14].

Evaluation Result

    Model Benchmark Prompt

    "Act as an approachable, supportive operational team leader[cite: 13, 14]. Draft a concise 3-sentence team channel reminder (Slack/Teams) asking the team to rinse and load their mugs into the dishwasher[cite: 13, 14]. Keep the tone warm, lighthearted, and focused on collective pride in our shared space, with a maximum length of 60 words[cite: 13, 14]."

    Notice how explicit boundaries prevent the AI from generating defensive rhetoric or formal reprimands[cite: 8, 14].
