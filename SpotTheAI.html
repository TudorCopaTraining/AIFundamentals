Activity 1: Spot the AI Generator

Evaluate the two communications below regarding expense submission deadlines. Determine which was generated by AI.

Sample A: "Hi Team, just a quick reminder that all mileage claims must be submitted by Friday at 4 PM to ensure processing for this month's payroll cycle. No late exceptions can be made. Thanks!"
Sample B: "Greetings valued colleagues! Please be cordially reminded that all vehicle transit reimbursement documentation must be filed prior to the conclusion of Friday's operational hours."
Not quite. Sample A shows direct, natural workplace tone and phrasing. Look closely at the corporate vocabulary density of Sample B.
Correct! Sample B exhibits classic signs of an unrefined prompt: overly formal wording ("cordially reminded", "vehicle transit reimbursement documentation") and robotic sentence structure.
