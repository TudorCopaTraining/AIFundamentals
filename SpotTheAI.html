Activity 01

Spot the AI: Communication & LLM Patterns

Identify classic LLM syntax hallmarks versus authentic human operational leadership.

Workplace Scenario: An operational deadline reminder regarding mileage claims needs to be issued to the team prior to Friday's payroll cutoff. Review the two communications drafted below.
Draft A

"Hi Team, just a quick reminder that all mileage claims must be submitted by Friday at 4 PM to ensure processing for this month's payroll cycle. No late exceptions can be made. Thanks!"

Draft B

"Greetings valued colleagues! Please be cordially reminded that all vehicle transit reimbursement documentation must be filed prior to the conclusion of Friday's operational hours."

Which text was generated by AI?

Incorrect. Draft A uses direct, conversational human cadence standard for workplace team leadership.
Correct! Draft B exhibits classic LLM hallmarks: unnatural over-formality ("cordially reminded", "vehicle transit reimbursement documentation") and robotic phrasing.

Human-in-the-Loop Principle: You remain personally accountable for any AI-assisted operational communication.
