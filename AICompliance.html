Activity 02

GDPR & Data Governance Gatekeeper

Public generative AI systems store inputs for model training. Classify each operational scenario as safe or a data breach.

Scenario 1: Pasting an anonymized list of 10 generic complaints (e.g., "Delivery was late", "Package was damaged") to identify recurring operational themes.

Safe: Customer names, addresses, and order references have been stripped. Analyzing generic phrasing does not expose PII.
Incorrect: Because personal identities and tracking numbers have been removed, this is a secure, standard automation workflow.

Scenario 2: Uploading your unit's official monthly financial spreadsheet to a public AI tool to draft budget bullet points for management.

Critical Breach: Public AI tools retain uploaded files on external third-party servers. Uploading internal financial metrics breaches corporate governance.
Correct: Serious compliance failure! Internal financial records and performance margins are proprietary business data and must never be uploaded to external public models.

Scenario 3: Pasting a draft handover email containing no staff names, client details, or account codes to check spelling and tone.

Safe: Checking syntax without including company secrets, staff names, or proprietary systems complies with acceptable workplace use.
Incorrect: Basic linguistic proofreading is safe provided all sensitive identifiers and organizational metrics are omitted.

Golden Rules: Never input customer PII or internal financial data into public models.
