Activity 2: Safe or Data Breach?

Decide whether each team action adheres to data governance or causes a breach.

1. Pasting 10 generic, anonymized customer complaints (e.g., "Delivery arrived damaged") into ChatGPT to identify recurring themes.

✅ Correct: Anonymized text with no personal identifiers (PII) is safe for thematic analysis.
❌ Incorrect: Because names, addresses, and tracking codes were removed, this is not a compliance breach.

2. Uploading the department's full Q3 internal budget spreadsheet to generate variance summaries.

❌ Critical Violation: Internal financial records uploaded to public web LLMs are stored externally and risk leakage.
✅ Correct: Major breach! Never upload proprietary financials or trade secrets to public AI models.

3. Submitting a drafted internal handover announcement that contains no personnel names, client details, or system passwords to check grammar.

✅ Correct: Using an LLM to check grammar and tone on non-sensitive drafts is an approved operational use case.
❌ Incorrect: De-identified communication copy does not expose confidential company data.
